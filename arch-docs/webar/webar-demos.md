Official WebAR.rocks Repositories (Main Organization)

WebAR.rocks/WebAR.rocks.face
Lightweight real-time face detection, tracking, and landmarks. Best for face filters, makeup VTO, glasses, necklaces, earrings, etc.
WebAR.rocks/WebAR.rocks.hand
Real-time hand & wrist tracking. Perfect for bracelets, watches, rings, and hand-based AR.
WebAR.rocks/WebAR.rocks.train
Train your own object detection / tracking models directly in the browser.
WebAR.rocks organization (main page)
Contains all their libraries (face, hand, object, etc.).
